Duck Creek Technologies has appointed Andy Moss as General Manager of Underwriting and Lloyd Peters as Vice President & General Manager, EMEA, following its acquisition of Send. The leadership appointments strengthen Duck Creek’s ability to advance agentic underwriting and support insurers across the London Market, specialty, commercial, and global insurance markets.
